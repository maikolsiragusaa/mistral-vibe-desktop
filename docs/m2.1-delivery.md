## Architecture (final)

- `git-service.js`: path-hardened mutations, per-repo serialization,
  stale-guarded hunk staging, app-managed worktree space
  (`userData/worktrees/<slug>-<sha1>/`), ownership-gated safe removal,
  read-only explorer/preview sources, `gh`-backed PR reads.
- `backend-pool.js`: `describeWorktreeUsage()` — frozen read-only ownership
  (session/worker/turn/subagent/lease/callbacks), unknown-blocks semantics.
- `preview-window.js` (new): dedicated isolated window (own partition,
  sandbox, no preload/Node), loopback-only URLs, same-origin redirect
  guards, block/error pages. The iframe is gone.
- Renderer: `git-types.ts`, `git-api.ts`, `panels/` (Changes, Files,
  History, Worktrees, PullRequests); `GitWorkspace.tsx` orchestrates state
  and effects only. `DiffViewer`, `git-diff.ts`, `preview-url.ts`,
  `subagent-history.ts` unchanged in role.
